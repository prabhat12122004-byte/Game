# Holi Warriors V20

A friendly, non-violent Holi water-balloon competition.

## Modes
- Single player: 2 AI bots by default.
- Multiplayer: room-code based connection; the Worker provides the room WebSocket.
- Three maps: Jungle, Sand, Space.
- Round times: 1, 2, 3, or 5 minutes.
- Each successful water-balloon hit = 10 points.
- A player goes out temporarily after 10 hits and returns after 10 seconds.

## Controls
- Left control: movement.
- Right control: 360-degree water-balloon aiming and automatic firing while held.

## Cloudflare
Deploy with `npx wrangler deploy`.
